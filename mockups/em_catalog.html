Request External Modules

Paid modules are billed monthly to your PTA after PI/Admin approval. Nothing is enabled or billed until your approver signs off.

Development projects: paid modules run free for the first 90 days after enabling. After 90 days the PTA is charged monthly regardless of project status — moving the project to Production starts charges immediately.
