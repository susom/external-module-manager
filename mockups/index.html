External Module Billing — UI Mockups

Today, paid REDCap External Modules are billed through R2P2 with manual bookkeeping. This proposal moves the whole lifecycle into REDCap itself: research teams request paid modules from a catalog, their PI approves the request and the PTA by email, approved modules are enabled automatically (or via an RSSD Jira ticket), a monthly invoice grouped by PTA goes to Finance as a CSV, Finance returns per-PTA results, and every unpaid charge is tracked as debt that re-bills automatically until it's paid or waived — so nothing falls through the cracks and backcharging is one click.

All seven screens below are clickable mockups with sample data — no backend. Tabs, carts, modals, lookups, validation states, and expand/collapse all work.
