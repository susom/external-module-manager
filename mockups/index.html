External Module Billing — UI Mockups

Today, paid REDCap External Modules are billed through R2P2 with manual bookkeeping. This proposal moves the whole lifecycle into REDCap itself: research teams request paid modules from a catalog, their PI approves the request and the PTA by email, approved modules are enabled automatically (or via an RSSD Jira ticket), a monthly invoice grouped by PTA goes to Finance as a CSV, Finance returns per-PTA results, and every unpaid charge is tracked as debt that re-bills automatically until it's paid or waived — so nothing falls through the cracks and backcharging is one click.

All eight screens below are clickable mockups with sample data — no backend. They render on top of the real REDCap 17.2.3 stylesheet (mirrored locally), so they match the live UI. Tabs, carts, modals, lookups, validation states, and expand/collapse all work.

Two billing policies captured in these mockups:
  1. Development projects get paid modules free for 90 days. After 90 days the PTA is charged monthly regardless of project status; moving to Production starts charges immediately.
  2. Every PTA carries an expiration date (captured at checkout). An alert emails the PTA contact and PIs two months before expiry to renew; if it lapses, the modules billed to it are disabled automatically.

The checkout survey (default REDCap survey theme): cart summary pre-filled from the request record, Stanford person-lookup to pick the PI/Admin approver, optional note, and the submitted confirmation.

4 — PI Approval Survey

A plain REDCap survey: request summary (with billing terms + PTA expiry), one approve/reject decision for the whole request, required rejection reason, signature.

Super user · Control Center
5 — Billing Runs

Monthly runs (draft → exported → finalized), run detail grouped PTA → project → EM line with carry-forward and dev-grace flags, audited overrides, plus the Generate tab.

Super user · Control Center
6 — Import Finance CSV

Upload the returned per-PTA CSV. All-or-nothing validation with a row-level error report; on success, statuses fan out to every line and the run finalizes.

Super user · Control Center
7 — PTA Accounts & Balances

PTA accounts with expiration dates + expiring/expired alerts, Project↔PTA mapping, and the backcharge ledger of what each PTA owes with attempt counts and age.

Super user · Control Center
8 — Request Admin & Reconciliation

All requests with per-EM statuses and Jira links, retryable enable errors, the nightly reconciliation queue, grandfather seeding, and the annotations injected into REDCap's native EM manager page.
